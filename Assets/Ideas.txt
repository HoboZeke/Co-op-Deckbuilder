Treasure:
You collect treasure cards which don't do anything but fill up your deck, however they count towards your meta-progress goals.

Cards:
		Freya lvl 3:
			Gain attack equal to the current shield of the active player
		Quick Study:
			Starts with 0 atk, when it kills something permenantly get +1 atk.
		Stubborn Elder:
			has 5 Strength, it's strength value can never be altered.
		Clumsy Jotun:
			Gains double the Str bonus from items, but destroys the item when it attacks.
		AshSteel Blade:
			Cannot be Destroyed.

Artifacts:
		Loyal Commander:
			-Increase all starting cards value by 1 and significantly improve their loot values if they are in your deck at the end of the voyage. (x2 to normal loot)
		Fancy Equipment:
			-Gain 1 recruit for every 2 items played in a round.
		Masterwork Plans:
			-All cards in play gain +1 attack when you have no cards in hand.
		Caltrops:
			-Any monsters taunted into your zone take 1 damage.
		Poisoned Club:
			-Stunned monsters will hit a random monster in their zone instead.
		Mirror Ship:
			-When picked up duplicated a copy of every warrior in your deck
		Mirror Vault:
			-When picked up duplicates a copy of every item in your deck
		The Mighty Pen:
			-When a warrior with 0 Strength attacks it hits for 3 damage
